About

Blade Rush is a frantic, fast paced top down shooter about fighting huge swarms of enemies, boosting all over the place, and trying not to dramatically explode.

Carve your way through a huge campaign of 30 levels, 5+ gamemodes and 3 epic bosses that want to ruin your day. Will you outlast against the swarm, or will you overheat?
